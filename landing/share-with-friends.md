# Share with friends — copy-paste messages

When sharing with friends, **honesty beats polish**. You're not selling — you're asking people
who trust you to take a look and tell you the truth. The best ask is "would you actually use this?"
not "please sign up." Send these 1:1 where you can; a personal note converts far better than a
group blast.

## Tips
- **Send 1:1 when it matters.** A direct "hey [name], you specifically..." beats any broadcast.
- **Ask for the opinion, not the signup.** "Would you use this?" gets replies; "please sign up"
  gets ignored. The signup happens naturally when the answer is yes.
- **Follow up the yeses.** Anyone who says "cool" → ask the 2 questions in message #4. These are
  your first real user interviews.
- **Don't over-send.** Pick the 10–20 friends whose judgment you actually trust. Quality > reach.
- Use `?ref=friends` so you can tell friend-signups apart from community traffic in Formspree.
